ABOUT SPT LEEDS

Your Trusted Mobile Parts Partner

Established in 2023, SPT Leeds (Smart Parts Traders) is a growing wholesale supplier of mobile phone parts and accessories, proudly serving local mobile phone repair shops, independent businesses and trade customers.

Since starting our business, we have built strong relationships with local repair shops and customers by focusing on dependable service, competitive trade prices and quality products.

We understand the demands of the mobile phone repair trade. Our customers need the right parts, competitive prices and reliable supply to keep their businesses moving. We aim to make sourcing mobile phone parts and accessories simple, convenient and dependable.

We continue to expand our range of mobile phone parts and accessories for repair businesses and retailers, while maintaining a strong focus on professional customer service and long-term relationships with our trade customers.
