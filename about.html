ABOUT SPT LEEDS

Your Trusted Mobile Parts Partner

Established in 2023, SPT Leeds (Smart Parts Traders) is a growing wholesale supplier of mobile phone parts and accessories, proudly serving local mobile phone repair shops, independent businesses and trade customers.

We understand what repair businesses need: reliable products, competitive trade prices and a dependable supply of parts for everyday repairs. Our aim is to make sourcing mobile phone parts simple, straightforward and reliable for businesses across Leeds and beyond.

From essential repair parts to mobile phone accessories, we are building long-term relationships with our customers by providing professional service, responsive support and products suited to the needs of the mobile repair trade.

Why Choose SPT Leeds?

  • Established in 2023 and growing with the local trade
  • Serving local mobile phone repair shops and businesses
  • Competitive wholesale and trade pricing
  • Quality mobile phone parts and accessories
  • Reliable supply and fast customer support
  • Focused on long-term customer relationships
