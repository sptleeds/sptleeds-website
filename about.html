ABOUT SPT LEEDS

Your Trusted Mobile Parts Partner

Established in 2023, SPT Leeds (Smart Parts Traders) is a growing wholesale supplier of mobile phone parts and accessories, proudly serving local mobile phone repair shops, independent businesses and trade customers.

Since starting our business, we have focused on building strong relationships with local customers and providing reliable access to the mobile phone parts and accessories they need for their day-to-day business.

We understand that repair businesses need dependable suppliers, competitive wholesale pricing and fast service. Our aim is to make sourcing mobile phone parts straightforward, reliable and convenient for our trade customers.
