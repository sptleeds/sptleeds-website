ABOUT SPT LEEDS

Your Trusted Mobile Parts Partner

Established in 2023, SPT Leeds (Smart Parts Traders) is a growing wholesale supplier of mobile phone parts and accessories, proudly serving local mobile phone repair shops, independent businesses and trade customers.

Since starting our business in 2023, we have been building strong relationships with local mobile phone repair shops and customers by focusing on dependable service, competitive trade prices and quality products.

We understand the demands of the mobile phone repair trade. Our customers need the right parts, competitive prices and reliable supply to keep their businesses moving. We aim to make sourcing mobile phone parts and accessories simple, convenient and dependable.

We supply a growing range of mobile phone parts and accessories for repair businesses and retailers. As our business continues to grow, our focus remains on providing professional customer service and developing long-term relationships with our trade customers.

Why Choose SPT Leeds?

  • Serving local mobile phone repair shops and trade customers
  • Competitive wholesale and trade pricing
  • Quality mobile phone parts and accessories
  • Reliable supply and responsive customer support
  • Professional service focused on long-term customer relationships
